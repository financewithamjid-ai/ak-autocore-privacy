AK AutoCore Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. About AK AutoCore

AK AutoCore is an automation and business management application designed to help authorized users manage connected social media pages and related business workflows.

2. Information We Access

When an authorized user connects a Facebook Page, AK AutoCore may access information permitted through Meta APIs, including Page identifiers, Page information, Page posts and content, engagement information, comments, and other Page-related information for which the user has granted permission.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide authorized features such as Page management, content publishing, engagement monitoring, comment management, workflow automation, and related business operations.

4. Facebook Permissions

Depending on features enabled by the authorized user, AK AutoCore may request Meta permissions required for Page discovery, Page content, publishing, engagement, and business-related Page management.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

AK AutoCore does not sell personal information or Meta Platform data. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or data brokerage purposes.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored application data and credentials. Access is limited to authorized users and systems.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain security and operational records, or meet applicable obligations.

8. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with AK AutoCore by contacting the application operator using the contact information below. After a valid request is received, applicable stored user data will be deleted unless retention is required for legitimate security or legal purposes.

9. Meta Platform Data

Data received from Meta is handled in accordance with applicable Meta Platform Terms and policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AK AutoCore functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the AK AutoCore operator through the contact email associated with the application.
