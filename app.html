AK AutoCore

Facebook Page Management & Social Media Automation

About AK AutoCore

AK AutoCore is a social media management system designed to help an authorized Facebook Page administrator manage their connected Page.

Facebook Integration

AK AutoCore uses Meta APIs to support authorized Page-management workflows including:

  • Identifying Facebook Pages managed by the authorized user
  • Publishing approved Page content
  • Reading Page posts and engagement information
  • Reading user-generated Page interactions such as comments
  • Supporting Page comment and engagement management

Authorization

AK AutoCore accesses only Facebook Pages for which the authenticated user has the required Page permissions. Facebook Platform Data is used only to provide the requested Page-management functionality.

Current Application Status

AK AutoCore is currently undergoing Meta App Review. Access to features requiring reviewed permissions is limited until Meta grants the applicable access.

Reviewer Information
